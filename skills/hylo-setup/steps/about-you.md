# Step: about-you

With few notes there's little to learn them from, so ask. What they tell you seeds their memory, and the news step uses it.

**Condition:** skip it if they have more than 20 notes, or `/_memory/_identity.md` already has items under `## Active`.

## Do

1. Ask, in one or two short sentences, what they do and what they're into lately. Make it feel like curiosity, not a form; give one example answer so it's easy to reply.
2. On their answer, react in one sentence that shows you got it. If they'd rather not say, skip the step.

## Record

1. Record what they told you as in the `hylo-memory` skill's "Record one fact" section, one item per fact:
   - who they are and what they do → `/_memory/_identity.md`
   - what they're working on or toward → `/_memory/_goals.md`
   - what they're into → `/_memory/_preferences.md`

   Don't add `pinned`: these change over time.
2. Mark `- [x] about-you — <a few words>`.
