# Step: channels

Let them know they can reach you away from this computer.

**Condition:** always. Nothing to ask: say it and go straight on to the next step in the same reply.

## Do

In one or two sentences: they can talk to you from WeChat, Discord or Telegram, usually from their phone, once connected in Settings → Server; and if they run Hylo on a home or cloud server, you stay reachable and bots keep running even when this computer is off (https://github.com/skoowoo/hylo/blob/main/docs/remote-deployment.md). If the intro guide already said this, keep it to one line.
