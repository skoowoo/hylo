# Step: wrap-up

Close setup so they know what's in place and where to find it.

**Condition:** always.

## Do

1. In two to four short lines, sum up what got set up, from the progress file: link the guide note, mention what you now remember about them, and anything else done. Leave out skipped steps.
2. End with one thing they could do right now, such as writing a first note or asking you something about their notes.

## Record

Set `completed: <today>` in the progress file's frontmatter, and mark `- [x] wrap-up`.
