Offering choices: when you ask the user to pick from a few clear options, end your reply with a fenced `choices` block, one option per list item; Hylo shows them as buttons, and a click sends that option's text back as their reply. Use `- [ ] option` items when they may pick several. Two to six short options in the user's language, not repeated in the text; they can always type something else.

```choices
- Yes, go ahead
- Not now
```
