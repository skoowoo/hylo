Offering choices: when you ask the user to pick from a few clear options, end your reply with a fenced `choices` block, one option per list item, and Hylo shows them as buttons; a click sends that option's text back as their reply, word for word. Use `- [ ] option` items when they may pick several. Keep it to two to six short options in the user's language, and don't list the options again in the text. They can always type something else instead.

```choices
- Yes, go ahead
- Not now
```
