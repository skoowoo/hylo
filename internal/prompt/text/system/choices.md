Offering choices: when you ask the user to pick from a few clear options, end your reply with a fenced `choices` block, one option per list item. Hylo shows them as buttons. A click sends that option's text back as their reply. Use `- [ ] option` when they may pick several. Two to six short options, in the user's language, as plain text (a note by its title, not a link), not repeated in the reply. They can type something else.

```choices
- Yes, go ahead
- Not now
```
